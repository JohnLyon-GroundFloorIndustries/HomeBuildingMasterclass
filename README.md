# Owner-Builder Masterclass

A step-by-step tutorial course from Ground Floor Industries for a family acting as its own general contractor on a single-family house, from the go/no-go decision through move-in or sale. 21 modules, 264 numbered steps.

**Live site:** https://johnlyon-groundfloorindustries.github.io/HomeBuildingMasterclass/

> **Draft. Not for construction.** No module has been reviewed by a working professional. Do not build from any of it until a builder, engineer, inspector, attorney and CPA have checked the parts that are theirs. Where the course and your stamped plans or local officials disagree, they govern.

## What is in it

- **Part A, Decide and plan (Modules 1–10):** go/no-go and choosing a track, legal structure and licensing, land, design, permitting, budget, financing, insurance and safety, schedule, managing the trades.
- **Part B, Build (Modules 11–18):** site work and utilities, foundation, framing and dry-in, exterior envelope, rough-in, insulation and drywall, interior finishes, exterior finish and landscaping.
- **Part C, Finish and exit (Modules 19–21):** final inspections and occupancy, closeout (move in or sell), after-action review.

Two tracks run through every module: owner-occupied (T1) and built for sale (T2).

The web page adds a one-page step map, step search, 27 figures and gate checklists.

## Assumptions the course is built on

- About 2,000 sq ft, single story, wood frame on a crawl space, all-electric with a heat pump, composition roof, fiber-cement siding.
- Unincorporated Kitsap County, Washington, on septic.
- Slab, basement, sewer and city-lot variants are noted where they change a step but are not written out.

## Status

As of 15 Nov 2026 this is a complete first draft, and the Markdown files match the web page.

The text went through one review round by five AI reviewers standing in for a builder, a structural engineer, a county inspector, a Washington construction attorney and a CPA. They proposed 125 corrections and 123 were applied. That is not a professional review. `course-index.md` lists the known limits, what is still unverified and the order in which licensed professionals should review the modules.

## Viewing and updating

No installation or build step. Use the live site, or open `index.html` in a browser. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so the page needs an internet connection to display as designed.

The site is published with GitHub Pages from the root of the `main` branch. A push to `main` updates it within a minute or two. When the course text changes, update `index.html` and the matching Markdown files together so they stay in step.
