# Module 21 — After-Action Review

**Draft v1 — 15 Nov 2026.** T1 owner-occupied, T2 spec.

**Outcome:** when you finish this module you will have a short written record of what the project really cost in money and time, what worked, what did not, and a decision about building again.

**Before you start:** baseline and final budget (6.8, 6.10), baseline and final schedule (9.8, 9.9), trade files (10.11), final cost report or profit-and-loss (20.6 or 20.15).

**When:** within 60 days of occupancy or sale, while memory is fresh. Half a day.

---

### 21.1 Compare budget to actual
- **What:** a table by cost code: baseline, final, difference, and the reason for each difference over 10%.
- **How:** sort the reasons into four groups: estimate was wrong; scope changed; price moved; mistake or rework.
- **Standard:** every contingency draw is explained.
- **Output:** budget variance table.

### 21.2 Compare schedule to actual
- **What:** a table by phase: planned duration, actual, difference, and cause.
- **How:** mark each delay as waiting on others (permits, inspections, deliveries, trades), weather, or your own pace.
- **Standard:** calculate your real multiple of professional duration for each phase. That number makes the next schedule honest.
- **Output:** schedule variance table.

### 21.3 Score trades and suppliers
- **What:** a scorecard for each: showed up as agreed, quality, passed inspection first time, price held, paperwork, would hire again.
- **How:** score 1–5 on each; add one sentence.
- **Output:** preferred list for the next project.

### 21.4 Count and value owner hours
- **What:** total hours by person and by phase, and what each hour earned or saved.
- **How:** total the hours from your log. For T1, labor savings are the fallback bids from 6.7, with sales tax, less what you spent in cash on the same scopes: materials, tools, rentals, paid helpers, and rework. Divide by hours. For T2, take profit after income tax and self-employment tax from 20.15 and divide by hours. Show the before-tax figure beside it.
- **Standard:** compare like with like. T1 savings are not taxed, so compare them to your take-home pay per hour. T2 profit is taxed, so compare the after-tax figure to take-home pay. Record what the project cost in family time. For T2 also record the return on the cash you had tied up, which is a separate question from the hourly figure [E].
- **Output:** owner labor summary.

### 21.5 Capture lessons
- **What:** one page: five things to repeat, five to change, and the three decisions that mattered most.
- **How:** each adult writes a list separately, then you compare.
- **Standard:** each lesson names the step number where it applies, so it can be acted on next time.
- **Output:** lessons-learned memo.

### 21.6 Decide on the next project
- **What:** a written decision: build again or not, which track, and under what conditions.
- **Options:** stop here; build another owner-occupied home later; move to a spec build with contractor registration; partner with a registered builder; hold the house as a rental (a different business with its own rules).
- **Standard:** the decision uses the real numbers from 21.1, 21.2, and 21.4, not the numbers you hoped for in Module 1.
- **Output:** decision memo, filed beside the first one.

## Top mistakes
1. Skipping the review because the house is finished.
2. Remembering the project as cheaper and faster than the records show.
3. Planning a spec build from the first project's best-case numbers.
